en

Pick a slot for consultation [here](https://drive.proton.me/urls/3ZH3T53DJM#2zonVR1fIZ7S).

- 1–3 Ideas/Concepts
- What does the Cyberdeck do?
- What can you do with it?
- Where and when?
- How does it feel? Which haptic, tactile, and olfactory qualities does the Cyberdeck have?
- Which materials do you need for it? (For the casing, (rotary) knobs, etc.?)
- How do you interact with the Cyberdeck (small buttons, giant buzzers, mini rotary knob, large control wheel, etc.)?
- How do ESP, components, and other materials come together?
- What do you want to learn? What do you need help with? What tools or workshop space do you need?
- You can work alone or in teams.
- We can only discuss what we can see or what you can show us. Bring drawings, mood boards, mock-ups, code, material samples, etc.
